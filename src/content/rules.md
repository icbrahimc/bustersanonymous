## Format

Busters Anonymous is a **12-team** head-to-head league split into two divisions —
**Booty Warriors** and **Titty Committee**. The regular season runs Weeks 1–14,
followed by a **6-team playoff** beginning Week 15.

- Head-to-head, one matchup per week.
- Division winners are guaranteed a playoff berth.
- Remaining playoff spots go to the best records league-wide (points-for breaks ties).

## Draft

- The draft is a **keeper draft** — each team may protect **1 keeper** before the draft.
- Standard draft plus keeper rounds as configured in Sleeper each season.
- Draft order is set by the commissioner before draft night.

## Waivers

- Waivers run on **FAAB** (Free Agent Acquisition Budget).
- Each team gets a **$200** budget for the season; minimum bid **$1**.
- Waivers process weekly; unclaimed players become free agents afterward.

## Trades

- Trades are reviewed and process after a short review period.
- **Trade deadline:** end of **Week 10**.
- Draft-pick trading is allowed. Collusion is not — see Conduct.

## Payouts

> Buy-in and payout amounts are set each season by the commissioner.

- 🏆 **League Champion** — first place in the playoff bracket.
- 🥈 **Runner-Up** — championship-game loser.
- 💰 **Regular-Season Points Leader** — most total points-for.

## Conduct

- Set a competitive lineup every week. Tanking and abandoned teams undermine the league.
- No collusion, dumping, or bad-faith trades.
- Keep the trash talk fun. This is a league of friends first.

---

*Rules are administered by the commissioner. Disputes are settled by a league vote
when the rulebook is silent.*
